# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, React, Vite, Python, FastAPI, Ultralytics YOLO11, PyTorch, Pillow

## Users
Farmers, agricultural field workers, growers, and crop protection advisors needing simple, instant, field-ready pest identification through leaf photos without technical complexity.

## Product Purpose
Empower farmers with an intuitive, trustworthy agricultural companion that identifies crop pests from smartphone photos or camera captures, explains damage symptoms in plain language, and provides safe, practical field remedies.

## Positioning
A farmer-first precision agriculture application powered by verified computer vision intelligence, presenting actionable crop care guidance with zero developer jargon or technical clutter.

## Operating Context
Outdoor agricultural fields, rural farmsteads, agricultural supply centers, and farm desks. Accessible on mobile browsers, tablets, and computers with direct camera support and high-visibility daylight design.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Capabilities: Seamless camera capture and image upload, instant pest identification with visual highlights, plain-language certainty scoring, severity alerts, organic and medicinal crop care protocols, visual pest encyclopedia for 8 primary crops, session-only history.
- Constraints: No technical AI jargon displayed to farmers; real YOLO11s inference preserved internally; strictly in-memory session history (no external database or user login).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: FasalMitra AI (फसल मित्र - Farmer's Crop Friend)
- Voice: Welcoming, practical, empowering, respectful of traditional farming wisdom.
- Visual Tone: Warm organic biophilic — deep forest greens, warm ivory canvas, muted sage, golden harvest accents, crisp legible typography.

## Evidence on Hand
- Pretrained model repository: Hugging Face `underdogquality/yolo11s-pest-detection`.
- Verified 102-class agricultural taxonomy with proven detection benchmarks.

## Product Principles
1. Farmer Simplicity First: Every label, button, and result must be immediately understandable to an ordinary grower.
2. Zero Technical Clutter: Hide model names, weights, tensor formats, and developer diagnostics from the user interface.
3. Actionable Agronomy: Pair every visual detection with immediate, practical steps (organic care, biological defense, and safe treatment).
4. Direct Camera Accessibility: Enable effortless photo capture directly through mobile and desktop web browsers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High-contrast text and cards optimized for outdoor daylight readability; large touch targets (minimum 48px) for mobile users with gloved or weathered hands; clear icons and plain language.
